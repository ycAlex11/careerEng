# Shopee Site Skill

## Site Policy

- Official entry: https://careers.shopee.cn/. Selectors, ATS handoffs, and application signals require live validation.
- Inherit shared retrieval, application history, pagination stopping, and safety rules. Do not invent site-specific limits or copy another employer's behavior.
- Record evidence-backed job URLs and native IDs. Resolve ambiguous identity with scoped identity tools, not assumptions about ID prefixes.

## Matching Policy

- Use current profile, career intent, CV, and shared matching policy. Do not substitute campus recruitment for experienced hiring without matching eligibility and intent.

## Session Preparation

- Discover authentication from the official career surface. Request user takeover for login, verification codes, or unavailable account details, preserving progress while waiting.
- Verify the actual account/application surface; a homepage redirect alone does not prove authentication.

## Channel Discovery

- Follow official navigation to jobs. Use external ATS destinations only when linked from the official site and retain handoff evidence.
- Confirm the destination belongs to Shopee and exposes actual jobs; do not confuse it with SHEIN or other employers.

## Apply

- Follow the frozen Apply List and shared policy. Inspect the current job page for already-applied or closed signals and persist confirmed outcomes.
- Use the batch-frozen CV artifact and verify attachment state. Do not assume a saved application contains the latest CV.
- Fill only supported profile facts; ask for missing material answers or required verification.
- Confirm submission through an explicit success signal or application record. Ambiguous results remain unresolved until verified; do not blindly repeat submission.
- Capture discoveries through existing evidence/evolution tools. Do not mark the Skill ready from one successful page load.
